# Round 2026-09-02 — owner's live-use report

Six problems reported from production use, with screenshots. This file is the working record:
what was observed, what the actual cause turned out to be, and the state of the fix. Cards are
deleted from `ROADMAP.md` when done; durable rules migrate into the relevant `docs/*.md`.

Status legend: `TODO` · `WIP` · `DONE` · `DESCRIBED-ONLY` (owner asked for a written spec, not code).

## 1. Auto-budget block: text overlapping, category names vanish — `DONE`

**Observed** (screenshot 1, `/plan` → «Автобюджет із історії»): two rows have no category name at
all, and their explanation sentence («Конверт перевищено 2 із 2 закритих місяців…») runs sideways
straight through the figures column, overlapping it.

**Cause — one defect, both symptoms.** `.ab-item` is `display: flex` (`styles/topbar.css`) and
`AutoBudget.tsx` renders FOUR direct children into it: the checkbox, `.bp-item-main`, and one or
two `.bp-reason` blocks. The reasons therefore became flex items in the SAME row instead of lines
under it. `.bp-item-main` is `flex: 1; min-width: 0`, so a long reason squeezes it toward zero and
`.bp-name` (which is `overflow: hidden`) collapses to nothing — the row loses its name. The rows
that looked "nameless" are exactly the two rows whose reason text is longest. Nothing was wrong
with the data.

**Fix.** The reason lines are moved inside a body wrapper so the row is `checkbox | body`, and the
body stacks main + reasons vertically. The layout can no longer put a sentence and a figure in the
same line regardless of text length.

## 2. AI budget plan: empty category, poor advice, no editing, cannot be dismissed — `DONE`

**Observed** (screenshot 2, `/plan` → «AI-план бюджету»):
1. a row with an empty name and «сер. 69 ₴ → 69 ₴»;
2. the plan budgets «Освіта» and «Податки» as if they were monthly, although both were last spent
   two months ago and the previous closed month was 0 in each;
3. the suggested amount cannot be edited in place, so accepting means accepting the model's number
   or nothing;
4. the block cannot be closed once read — it stays open forever on the page.

**Causes.**
1. `proposeBudgets` (`worker/lib/ai/budget.ts`) groups by `EFF_CAT_ID` with no `IS NOT NULL`
   filter, so **uncategorised spending** becomes a row with `category_id = NULL` and a NULL name.
   An envelope cannot be created for it anyway, so it is noise in a list of proposals.
2. The payload gives the model ONE number per category — the canonical monthly level — and no
   sense of *shape*. A quarterly tax and a monthly rent look identical in it. The level itself is
   correct (§LEVEL-WINDOW divides by covered months), but "3 402 ₴/mo" for a tax paid once a
   quarter invites a monthly envelope for something that will not be charged this month. The model
   needs the same distinction the app already computes for itself: §BURN-SHAPE knows which part of
   burn is recurring and which is lumpy, and `budgetStatus`/`budget_months` know how many of the
   last closed months the category was active at all.
3/4. UI only.

**Done.**
- The NULL category is filtered out of both `proposeBudgets` and `budgetChatReply`.
- The payload now carries the SHAPE of each category, not only its size: `active_months`
  (of `months_of_history`, the §LEVEL-WINDOW denominator, via the new `levelWindowKeys` export),
  `lumpy` (§BURN-SHAPE's own verdict, so the plan and the burn breakdown cannot disagree) and
  `fixed`, plus `monthly_burn_recurring_uah` on the top level. The prompt states in as many words
  that a lumpy or half-active category must not be budgeted as if it were charged every month,
  and must say which choice it made in `reason`.
- The suggested amount is an inline input; the delta % is recomputed from the EDITED figure and
  the edited figure is what gets saved, per row and for "accept all". The footer totals the
  edited plan.
- «Згорнути» collapses the whole block, remembered in `localStorage` (a per-browser convenience,
  not account data).

## 3. Goals: a jar collected in USD is displayed and judged in UAH — `DONE`

**Observed** (screenshot 3): goal «На ланос», linked to a monobank jar the owner funded in
**dollars**, reads «4 480 ₴ з 2 000 ₴ · 100% · Ціль досягнута 🎉».

**Cause.** A goal has no currency of its own. The jar balance is converted into the display base
(`toBaseMinor`, `routes/api/goals.ts`), while `target_amount` is a user-typed figure and therefore
stored in hryvnia by §BASE-CUR. So the target the owner typed as **$2 000** was stored as
**2 000 ₴** and is now compared against a converted balance. The comparison is arithmetically
consistent and factually wrong: the goal is ~5% complete and the app is congratulating him.

**Done — §GOAL-CUR, migration 0048.** `savings_goals.currency_code`, NULL meaning "hryvnia", so
every existing manual goal is untouched; the migration stamps jar-linked goals with their jar's
currency and deliberately leaves the stored NUMBER alone (re-labelling 2 000 as $2 000 is what the
owner meant when he typed it).

- `goalCurrency()` (`lib/finance/goals.ts`) is the single answer to "which currency is this goal
  in" — the jar's when a jar backs it, the column otherwise, hryvnia for legacy rows.
- `/goals`, `/goals/:id/progress` and `/goals/:id/contributions` return every figure in the goal's
  own currency, unconverted, and each response says which currency that is. The pace ratio now
  compares two numbers that are in the same unit by construction.
- Writes store what was typed, in the goal's currency; re-pointing a goal at a jar in a different
  currency CONVERTS what is already stored (`convertMinorBetween`) rather than silently
  re-labelling it.
- Two other readers were converting a JAR BALANCE as if it were hryvnia and were wrong by the
  whole exchange rate: `draftGoalRisk` (the feed) and the AI event close-out. Both now go through
  `goalCurrency` and convert into the base once, at the edge, where §BASE-CUR requires it.
- Held by `worker/test/goal-currency.test.ts` (4 scenarios). The fixture contains **no goals at
  all**, which is why none of this was ever caught: `__golden__/goals.json` is an empty array.
- `draftGoalRisk` moved to `lib/messaging/drafts-goals.ts` under lint C3, next to the budget,
  import and AI drafters split off for the same reason.

## 4. Cashflow calendar: design and month navigation — `DONE`

**Observed** (screenshot 4): the grid reads as a table of bordered boxes rather than a calendar;
month switching is two small chevrons split apart by the month label, and the window is limited to
"this month / next month" with no way back.

**Done.** No calendar library was added: each one ships its own visual language that would have to
be overridden back into this design system's tokens, and the artifact CSP forbids external
stylesheets in any case. What the block needed was design work, and it got it:

- the grid is one canvas with hairline separators instead of 35 individually bordered boxes;
- leading and trailing days of the neighbouring months are drawn muted rather than left as holes,
  so the first row no longer starts in mid-air;
- today is marked on the day NUMBER (a pill), because a ring around the cell competed with the
  ring that means "liquidity dip" and the two signals had become one;
- the day total is a pill, green for a net inflow (§INCOME-PLAN: income arrives as a negative
  amount, and a payday drawn in red was the earlier bug in this same block);
- weekends are tinted, weekday letters are uppercase and aligned to the columns;
- navigation is one control — ‹ month › with the chevrons actually rotated into arrows (they were
  both pointing the same way) — plus a «Сьогодні» button that appears only when it would do
  something.
- The window is three months forward instead of two. It is fetched WHOLE, in one request, because
  the cushion projection is a running subtraction across the payload — asking per month would
  restart the balance at each month's first day and report a dip that never happens. The client
  stops at the edge of what the server sent rather than paging into empty months, which would read
  as "no more charges ever".
- The block moved into its own `src/styles/calendar.css` (C8: `domains-a.css` hit its ceiling
  again, and an exception may never rise). It is imported at the exact point its rules previously
  occupied, so nothing about the cascade order changed.

## 5. Advice history: too long, no per-row delete — `DONE`

**Observed** (screenshot 5): every stored snapshot is listed (up to 24), which buries the recent
ones; only «Очистити» exists, which is all-or-nothing.

**Done.** The list shows the last 5 with a «Показати всі (N)» toggle, and each row has its own
delete. `DELETE /advisor/history/:at` addresses one entry by its `generated_at` — the only
identity these rows have, since they live as a JSON array in `app_state` and an index would shift
under a concurrent delete from another device. Deleting an entry that is already gone is a no-op,
not an error. The runway chart keeps the FULL series: trimming a list must not trim a trend.

The storage moved to `worker/lib/ai/advice-history.ts` (lint C3 — `advisor.ts` was at its
exception, and an exception may never rise) and is re-exported from `advisor.ts`, so no caller
changed. Same argument that took the §A1 facts out of that file.

## 6. Budget page: category name should link to the category page — `DONE`

**Done.** Envelope names and AI-plan rows link to `/categories/:id`. Styled to keep reading as a
figure row — no underline until hover — because an underlined blue name in a row of numbers reads

⚠️ The auto-budget rows are deliberately NOT links: each row is a `<label>` wrapping its checkbox,
and a link inside a label is activated by the browser as the label, so clicking the name would
both navigate and silently toggle the row out of the plan.

## 7. AI should categorise operations it clearly can — `DONE` (§AI-CATCHUP)

**Observed** (screenshot 6): transaction «Списання відсотків за серпень», MCC 6012, sits in
«Перекази і зняття» with `real_category_id` empty, while four operations with the same meaning
(«Відсотки по кредиту», «Списання відсотків за травень», …) are already filed under «Відсотки по
кредиту та схоже», a category the owner has. The name describes the operation perfectly and the
app did not apply anything.

**Why it happens.** `categorize()` stops at the MCC rule: 6012 → bucket 13 («Перекази і зняття»),
which is a correct answer to "what kind of banking operation is this" and no answer at all to
"where did the money go". `real_category_id` is exactly the field for the second question and
nothing fills it automatically here. The merchant-consensus step (≥3 charges under one root, ≥80%
agreeing) does not fire either, because the bank varies the description every month («за серпень»,
«за травень», «Відсотки по кредиту»), so `coreToken` produces a different root each time — the
very case §SUB-FIND fixed for search by widening the haystack, not applied here.

**Built on 2026-09-02 as described** — `worker/lib/ai/catchup.ts`, wired as the `catchup` step of
the daily cron pass, ahead of `notifications` (the feed's «N операцій без категорії» asks the
person to finish work only they can finish, and asking them to do what the app was about to do
itself minutes earlier wastes their attention on its own backlog). Full reasoning in
`docs/AI.md` §AI-CATCHUP. Held by `worker/test/catchup.test.ts` — 9 scenarios, and eight of them
are refusals, because the happy path is the cheap half of a pass that runs unattended.

⚠️ One thing the diagnosis above got slightly wrong, corrected by reading the code: the machinery
for bucket 13 already existed (`categorizeTransfers`, §F2 step 2). What was missing was not the
categoriser but the pass that NOTICES the gap — and that it asks once, for everything at once,
rather than once per row behind a button nobody presses.

⚠️ `real_category_id` became the fourth §AI-AUDIT field on the way. The model had been writing it
since July, and it was the one such field with no journal and no undo.
